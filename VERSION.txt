The Blueprint Workout App
Version: 1.0.0
Release date: 2026-09-08

Included capabilities
- Full W1-W12 training prescriptions with programmed set and rep changes
- Weight, reps, and RIR entry for every working set
- Previous-performance and next-session progression suggestions
- Editable exercises, alternatives, rest times, increments, and muscle credits
- Planned and completed weekly credited reps/sets by muscle and muscle group
- Offline refocus-cycle builder for future 12-week blocks
- Gym calendar, manual visit logging, whole-session timer, rest timer
- Weekly, monthly, yearly, and all-time gym-time totals
- Body weight, measurements, calories, and macro targets
- JSON backup/restore and Excel-friendly CSV exports
- Responsive phone layout and offline PWA cache
